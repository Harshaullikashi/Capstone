# Capstone 
Walmart Sales Forecasting:
 A nationwide retail store with multiple outlets is struggling to manage inventory efficiently due to unpredictable demand
 patterns. The business requires a forecasting model to help anticipate sales trends across individual stores to better
 align supply with demand.
  To analyze historical weekly sales data and develop time series forecasting models for each store, predicting sales for
 the upcoming 12 weeks. Additionally, to derive actionable insights that could guide inventory and operational decisions.
 
Online Retail Customer Segmentation:
 An online retail store wants to understand its customer purchasing behavior to improve marketing and operational
 efficiency. The company has transactional data from thousands of purchases and seeks to segment customers based
 on their value and engagement.
  To preprocess transaction data and apply customer segmentation techniques such as RFM analysis and K-Means
 clustering to identify distinct customer groups. The output helps with personalization, retention strategies, and marketing
 efforts.
